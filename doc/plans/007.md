# Plan 007: The librarian's cataloging surfaces (progressive enrichment)

> **This is a from-scratch rewrite.** The committed "Discovery workflow" (commit
> `b944570`) was built on a wrong model — one big form that demanded facts the librarian
> doesn't have at intake (first-publication, book-wide `exposure`), conflated FRBR levels
> and bounded contexts, and hard-coded row counts. It is replaced, not patched. The
> domain this plan implements is documented in
> [`doc/contributors/librarian-cataloging.md`](../contributors/librarian-cataloging.md);
> read it first.

## Context

Plans 001–005 built the five contexts' model layers; 006 shipped the public read-only
library; plan-007-as-committed added a write surface that missed the workflow. A working
session cataloging real books (the "case law" in the contributor doc) established what the
librarian actually does:

**Record a fact you found — a title alone is enough — then enrich it on the entry, later,
piece by piece.** Progressive enrichment (plan 004's "failure isn't a thing — it's just
how far it goes"), not a form to fill. The surface used to *add more later* is the same
one used while first recording, and existing entries are easy to surface and change.

Hard constraints (from the owner, grounded in the session):

- **Deterministic web app, no LLM.** Ordinary forms and small focused actions, following
  Cooper (*About Face*) and Tidwell (*Designing Interfaces*). Deterministic conveniences
  only (e.g. derive the source from a URL host). The role-play pastes (RIS, MARCXML,
  mastheads, ©-pages) taught the *kinds of data* the librarian has — **not** an
  auto-parsing feature.
- **Never demand a missing fact; never invent one.** Absent, not defaulted. Create
  components only when the librarian enumerates/counts them.
- **Correctness is the bar** — the full, correct FRBR graph and correct context seams,
  never a degenerate model. "Minimal effort" is a property of the *interaction*, never a
  licence to persist wrong data.

The five-context model already exists; **this plan is mostly the librarian's write
surfaces on top of it, plus a few model corrections and the copyright verdict.**

## Model corrections (small; most of the model already exists)

### Magic — kill the `exposure` misnomer (owner decision "A")
`exposure` (`public`/`protected`, default `public`) is wrong: exposure is **per-concept,
not book-wide**, and "public" conflates copyright-PD with openly-teachable (see
`magic-taxonomy-research.md`). Correct it honestly:
- `magic_listings.exposure` becomes **nullable — "unclassified"** (drop the `public`
  default). No book carries a fabricated access class.
- **Visibility ≠ downloadability** (plan 006). Listings stay browsable/searchable
  regardless. **External read-links (HathiTrust/PG/LoC/IA) are never gated** — they are
  public-domain or publicly-readable editions; the library always links out.
- The access gate applies only to **our own produced downloads**, and treats
  `unclassified` as **not open**. (Barely bites yet: we produce no downloads until the
  digitization pipeline runs.) Rework `Magic::Listing#accessible_to?` accordingly.
- Per-concept exposure classification is the deferred trick-level model, untouched here.

### Catalog — name-as-used vs. best-name
Support "*Thavant* by Phil Goldstein, *Parallax* by Max Maven, both browse under **Max
Maven**." `Catalog::Agent#name` is the **best name** (collocation). Add an optional
`Catalog::Contribution#nomen` (→ `Catalog::Nomen`) = the **name as used** on that
contribution; display falls back to `Agent#name` when absent. Per-edition byline still
also lives transcribed in `Manifestation#statement_of_responsibility`. *(Legal identity —
unifying pen names to the natural person, heirs, chain-of-title — is **Copyright's**, per
IDDD, referenced by ID; not built here beyond the existing plan-003 surface.)*

### Copyright — the computed verdict (compute, never demand)
When the librarian records a first-publication/notice **year** on a work, compute the
verdict by **date math against today**, extending the existing
`Copyright::Determination::TermExpiration`:
- `year ≤ this_year − 95` → **public domain** (`determined`, `term_expired`,
  `public_domain_on` in the past).
- `year ≥ 1978` → **not public domain** (`determined`; a precise PD date may be
  uncomputable without a death date — record the verdict, not a fake date).
- `(this_year − 95) < year < 1978` → **needs Rule 6** (`researching`).
No verdict is offered when no year is given (the exterior-photo case). Renewal/notice
research, heirs, and chain-of-title remain the separate **Rule 6** track (plan 003).

### Kept as-is (already committed, correct)
`catalog_works.form_of_work_id` nullable; `Catalog::ExternalReference` (a record *about* a
work — shared evidence, promotable to a Copyright citation); `catalog_manifestations.
access_address` + the "Online resource" carrier; the book page reading Catalog editions;
first-run granting the founder every role. **Keep all of these.**

### FRBR relationship mappings (documentation, mostly no new schema)
The existing relationship vocabulary covers the case law; the plan records the mapping so
implementers use it consistently:
- **Volume / issue / installment** → `Catalog::WorkRelationship` kind `part` (dependent
  parts); issue/volume numbering on `Manifestation#edition_or_issue` + `series`.
- **Collects / omnibus / collected column** → an **aggregate manifestation** embodying the
  component works' expressions (the M:N `Embodiment` from plan 001) + an **aggregating
  work**. Not a work-to-work "collects" kind.
- **Reproduction** (image scans of a print) → `Catalog::ManifestationRelationship` kind
  `reproduction`.
- **Transcription** (PG) → its own digital-text `Manifestation` of the same expression.
- **Derivation** (original Tarbell course → hardcovers) → shared lesson-works via
  `Embodiment` where composition is known; a container-level `WorkRelationship` where it
  isn't ("state the relationship at the higher level when the lower level is unknown").
- **Serial** (Genii) → a serial `Work`; issues as dependent parts / issue-manifestations.
  If a serial indicator is needed beyond `form_of_work`, add one small `Work` attribute
  (decided in implementation; FRBR "intended termination").

## The librarian surfaces (the bulk of the work)

Purpose-driven, RESTful, **small focused resources** — each a tiny controller calling one
intention-revealing model method, guarded by `LibrarianAccess` (`can_catalog?`). No
generic admin; no god-form. This is the "add more, on-the-fly, later" surface and the
"record it the first time" surface — the same components.

- **Create an entry from a title.** `POST` a `Work` with just a title → redirect to that
  entry's workbench. That is the whole "record a discovery."
- **The entry workbench** (`works#show` for librarians) — shows everything known and
  offers a focused action per facet, each optional:
  - **Add an edition** (a `Manifestation`): print or digital; for digital, derive the
    source from the URL host (PG/HathiTrust/LoC/IA) and set the online carrier +
    `access_address`; choose *reproduction of* an existing edition or *transcription*.
  - **Add a creator** (a `Contribution`): role (created/wrote/edited/illustrated/…), the
    **name as used**, linked to an author-identity (**best name**) via a deterministic
    search picker — never auto-merged.
  - **Relate** to another entry: part-of / issue-of / installment-of / collects /
    reproduction / derivation — via a picker over existing entries.
  - **Record a copyright notice** (claimant, year, registration) → the computed **verdict**
    appears (PD / not PD / needs Rule 6).
  - **Cite an external reference** (Magicpedia, dealer page, review) — shared evidence.
  - **Add a subject** (a `Concept`, or a **person-as-subject** linking the same
    author-identity used elsewhere) and **variant/parallel/half titles**.
- **Find & edit** existing entries: a librarian catalog search (extend
  `Magic::Listing.matching`/a Catalog search) for cross-linking pickers and for opening an
  entry to change it. Collocation is by author **best name**.

UI specifics (layout, control patterns) follow the frontend skill + Cooper/Tidwell at
implementation time; this plan fixes the **resources, the data each captures, and the
model**, not pixels — deliberately, to avoid re-guessing interaction detail.

## Tests
Model + request tests (Minitest + fixtures, real login) proving, against the **case law**:
a title alone creates an entry; an omnibus of enumerated components; a multi-volume set
of a stated count (no invented gaps); print + reproduction + transcription editions of one
work with correct relationships; two same-surnamed people not merged (Maskelyne); one
serial with several issues; a column-as-series plus its collected book cross-linked to
existing entries; ©-notice → each verdict (PD / not PD / needs Rule 6) by date math;
external references as shared evidence; name-as-used vs best-name collocation; and the
entries appearing on the public site, searchable, with ungated external read-links.

## Scope discipline (explicitly NOT here)
- **Items / holdings / provenance** — our copies, other libraries' copies, donors: out
  (irrelevant to the catalog).
- **The digitization Workflow** — a separate, deliberate "begin digitization" action on
  any entry (external scans are eligible sources; PG short-circuits into the
  Standard-Ebooks ePub track). Not intake.
- **Deep Copyright Rule 6** — heirs, renewal/notice research, corporate chain-of-title,
  the legal person-unification: the separate researcher track (plan 003). This plan only
  wires the automatic term verdict.
- **Per-concept exposure / the trick-level categorization** — deferred
  (`magic-taxonomy-research.md`).
- **LLM paste-parsing** — never.

## Owner decisions (resolved)
- Progressive enrichment; **a title alone creates an entry**; enrich later on the same
  surface; find/edit existing.
- Deterministic UI, no LLM; source-from-URL for known hosts.
- FRBR is the structure vocabulary; create components only when enumerated.
- **Names/identity are authoritative in Copyright** (legal, unified to the natural person,
  heirs, chain-of-title); **Catalog keeps best-name-for-browse + name-as-used**; the two
  are separate models linked by ID (IDDD); their canonical names may differ.
- Copyright verdict computed by date math (`this_year − 95`), never demanded; **A**:
  exposure unclassified, no misnomer.
- The smallest usable slice = **capture everything from the session, fully editable, on the
  public site, searchable, with external read-links** — built correct-and-whole, no
  shortcut.
